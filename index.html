Our Selection Rationale

Not Just a Supervisor. The Person Who Sets the Pace for Rubicon's Service Standard.

Rubicon operates across 100,000+ service locations with a vendor and hauler network that demands coordination, accountability, and precision. The Client Services Team Lead is the person who ensures that the frontline team delivers the transparency, responsiveness, and quality that Rubicon promises to every client. This is a leadership role that requires someone who can manage people, optimize processes, and handle escalations without losing sight of the bigger picture.

We screened for candidates with proven team leadership experience in high-volume service environments: BPO operations, contact centers, and multi-channel support teams. We prioritized people who have managed KPIs, coached agents, and built accountability structures. Every candidate in this batch has led teams of 10 or more, speaks fluent English, and understands the rhythm of U.S.-facing client operations.

We also weighted heavily for process improvement instincts. Rubicon's data-driven service model needs a team lead who can translate metrics into action, not just report numbers. These are operators who think in systems, not just tasks.

What we screened for: Team leadership in service delivery or BPO environments, KPI management and performance coaching, escalation handling, CRM/ticketing system proficiency, workforce management experience, English fluency (C1+), and a demonstrated ability to improve team output and quality metrics over time.
